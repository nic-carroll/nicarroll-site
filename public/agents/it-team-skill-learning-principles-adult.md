# Design for Adult Learners

## When to Use

Use whenever you design, choose, or review training, a course, or a tool for working adults and adult education students.

## Steps

1. Explain why it matters. Adults want to know the reason before they invest the time.
2. Respect their experience. Draw it out and build on it. Never teach as if they know nothing.
3. Give choice and control: self paced options, the ability to test out, and a say in goals.
4. Center on real problems and tasks from their work or life, not on subjects.
5. Make it usable right away. If they cannot apply it this week, reconsider the timing.
6. Respect their time: short, focused, and available when and where they can learn.
7. Remove barriers such as schedule, cost, technology access, language, and fear of failing in front of others.
8. Treat them as peers. Facilitate more than you lecture.
9. Follow up on the job, where the learning is put to use.

## Output

A design or review that states the audience and shows how each principle was applied.
