# Learning Tool Evaluation

## When to Use

Use before recommending, buying, or approving any app, platform, or digital resource for learning.

## Steps

1. State the learning need the tool is meant to meet.
2. Check whether a tool already approved does the job. If so, stop here.
3. Judge the fit for the learners. Use the learning principles skill for your sector.
4. Review privacy: what data is collected, who gets it, and whether an agreement is signed. Hand off to the privacy and app vetting agents.
5. Review accessibility. Hand off to the Accessibility Coordinator Agent.
6. Check sign on, rostering, support, and the full cost over the life of the tool.
7. Pilot with a small group and collect evidence.
8. Decide to approve, approve with conditions, or decline, and publish the decision with the reason.

## Output

A tool evaluation with a decision and reasons, added to the approved or denied list.
