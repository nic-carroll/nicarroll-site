# Design for K12 Learners

## When to Use

Use whenever you design, choose, or review a lesson, course, tool, or resource for children and teenagers in school.

## Steps

1. Match the learner's age and stage. What works in grade 2 does not work in grade 10.
2. Align to the standards and the adopted curriculum for the grade and subject.
3. Build in structure and scaffolds: clear steps, models, guided practice, then independent work.
4. Keep the teacher in the lead. Tools support instruction. They do not replace the teacher's judgment.
5. Plan for short attention and active work: frequent checks, movement between activities, and feedback right away.
6. Design for every learner: reading level, language, disability, and access to devices and internet at home.
7. Protect students. Collect as little data as possible, follow student privacy law, and keep adults in supervision of any online interaction.
8. Include families. They need to understand what is being used and why.
9. Use motivation that builds skill and belonging, not only points and prizes.

## Output

A design or review that states the grade band and shows how each principle was applied.
