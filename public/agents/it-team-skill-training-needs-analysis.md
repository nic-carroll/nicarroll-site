# Training Needs Analysis

## When to Use

Use when someone asks for training, to confirm training is the right answer and what it should cover.

## Steps

1. Ask what problem the requester is trying to solve.
2. Describe what people do now and what they should do.
3. Find the cause: knowledge and skill, or process, tools, time, and motivation.
4. If the cause is not knowledge or skill, say so and hand off to the process improvement review skill.
5. Describe the learners: who they are, what they already know, and when they can learn.
6. List the few tasks that matter most.
7. Recommend the lightest solution that will work, including a job aid or no training at all.
8. State how success will be measured.

## Output

A needs analysis with the cause, the audience, the priority tasks, and a recommended solution.
