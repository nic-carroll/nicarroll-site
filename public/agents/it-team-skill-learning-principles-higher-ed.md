# Design for Higher Education Learners

## When to Use

Use whenever you design, choose, or review a course, tool, or resource for college and university students.

## Steps

1. Know the students. Many are adults who work, commute, care for family, or study fully online.
2. Respect academic freedom. Faculty decide what and how they teach. You advise and support.
3. Start from course and program learning outcomes, and align activities and assessments to them.
4. Teach students to manage their own learning: clear expectations, a steady weekly rhythm, and early feedback.
5. Use the practices of the discipline. A lab, a studio, a clinic, and a seminar teach differently.
6. Build active learning and real interaction with the instructor and with peers.
7. Design for access from the start: accessible materials, low cost resources, and flexibility where it does not lower the standard.
8. Protect student records and academic integrity without treating students as suspects.
9. Use evidence of student learning to improve the course and the program.

## Output

A design or review that states the student population and shows how each principle was applied.
