# Professional Learning Design

## When to Use

Use when planning training or professional development for adults, including teachers, faculty, and staff.

## Steps

1. Start from what people need to do differently, not from a topic.
2. Find the gap between current and wanted practice, with evidence.
3. Write outcomes you could observe on the job.
4. Design active practice using the learners' own work, lessons, or cases.
5. Use short sessions spread over time, not one long event.
6. Plan the follow up: coaching, job aids, or a check in.
7. Measure whether people applied it, not whether they attended.
8. Revise or stop based on what you find.

## Output

A professional learning design with outcomes, practice activities, follow up, and a measure of application.
