# Efficiency Audit

## When to Use

Use when you want to find where time, money, or effort is being spent without a matching result.

## Steps

1. List the recurring tasks for the role and the hours each takes per month.
2. Rank by total hours, then by how repetitive the task is.
3. For the top five, ask: can it be stopped, combined, delegated to another agent, or automated?
4. Check for duplicate work with other roles by asking the lead agent who else does something similar.
5. Estimate hours saved and what is needed to get there.
6. Pick the two changes with the best return and plan them.

## Output

A ranked list of time sinks with a recommended action and hours saved for each.
