# Coaching Cycle

## When to Use

Use when working one on one or with a small team to change teaching or training practice.

## Steps

1. Let the person being coached set a goal tied to what their learners will do.
2. Gather a starting point together: observe, or look at learner work.
3. Plan one change. One, not five.
4. Model it, teach alongside, or observe while they try it.
5. Reflect together on evidence of what learners did, not on opinions.
6. Decide the next step: repeat, extend, or close the cycle.
7. Keep it confidential. Coaching is never used to evaluate the person coached.

## Output

A completed cycle with the goal, the change tried, the evidence, and the next step.
