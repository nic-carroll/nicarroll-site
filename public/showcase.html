NextGen SIS

A full student information system for K12 districts

One system for the whole school day: student records, enrollment, classes, attendance, gradebook, scheduling, student support, family access, and data checks for state reporting. Districts run it in their own cloud and get updates from one hub. Every screen here uses invented students from a fictional district.

  • Records and enrollment
  • Attendance and gradebook
  • Master scheduler
  • Student support
  • Family and student portal
  • Form builder
  • OneRoster 1.1 and 1.2 export
  • Audit log

EduSite

One accessible platform for every district and school site

A low cost website platform that runs a district site and every school site from one dashboard. The district sets the menu and look once, schools publish inside it, and an accessibility checker blocks publishing when a page has errors. Shown here on the made up Oakhaven County School District.

  • WCAG 2.1 AA
  • Section 508
  • One dashboard for every site
  • Publishing workflow
  • AI assistant for staff updates
  • Audit log

EagleAudit

Every tool, one view

EagleAudit connects security systems with read only access, runs every check on a schedule, and puts the evidence in front of the people who decide. It covers devices and tenants, identity and access, activity and threats, AI oversight, and risk and compliance.

  • Built in house with AI
  • Nightly checks
  • Read only access
  • NIST 800-171
  • NIST CSF
  • CIS Controls
AI speeds up the work. People still make every decision.

Donor Email

See when donors open your emails

A Gmail add on and team dashboard for fundraising staff. Gift officers send personal emails from their own Gmail and see when each donor opens them, and managers see the whole team. It ships with a picture guide so staff can set it up on their own.
