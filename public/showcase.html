EagleAudit

Every tool, one view. EagleAudit connects security systems with read only access, runs every check on a schedule, and puts the evidence in front of the people who decide. It covers devices and tenants, identity and access, activity and threats, AI oversight, and risk and compliance.

  • Built in house with AI
  • Nightly checks
  • Read only access
  • NIST 800-171
  • NIST CSF
  • CIS Controls

AI speeds up the work. People still make every decision.

Policy Library

One place for every policy, from the first need to the next review. Leaders see policy health at a glance, reviewers work in the document itself with tracked changes, and every sign off is recorded.

Change Management Review

A review of how IT changes are requested, approved, and scheduled, built from two years of agendas and request records. Staff answer a short survey and post ideas on a shared board, and the results update as people respond.

  • Process map
  • Agenda analysis
  • Request records
  • Discussion board
  • Live survey results

Donor Email

A Gmail add on and team dashboard I built for fundraising staff. Gift officers send personal emails from their own Gmail and see when each donor opens them, and managers see the whole team. It ships with a step by step picture guide so staff can set it up on their own.

  • Gmail add on
  • Team dashboard
  • Open tracking
  • Roles for officers and managers
  • Picture guide
