02 // logo rules

How to Use the Mark

DO
  • Keep clear space equal to one quarter of the width of the mark
  • Use the white N with the accent loop on navy or dark photos
  • Use the navy N with the accent loop on white or light backgrounds
  • Keep the primary mark at 24 pixels or larger, the Signature N at 16, and the lockup at 160 wide
DO NOT
  • Stretch, rotate, recolor, outline, or add shadows
  • Place the mark on a busy photo without a navy or white tile
  • Put the mark inside a circle or ring
  • Retype the N in a font or redraw the mark

05 // type

Type

HEADLINES AND NUMBERS

Bricolage Grotesque

Weight 700 with tight letter spacing, about minus 2 percent.

BODY AND LABELS

Public Sans keeps long text calm and easy to read at any size.

Weights 400, 500, and 600. Body is 17 pixels with a 1.65 line height on the web.

Both fonts are open source under the SIL Open Font License and are included in the package.

06 // voice

Voice and Writing

HOW IT SOUNDS
  • Plain words and short sentences that lead with the result
  • Title Case for headings and buttons
  • Commas, periods, or the word "to" in place of dashes
  • Outcomes a board cares about: leading, saving money, delivering at scale, and security
WHAT STAYS TRUE
  • Every number is true and sourced
  • Planned work is never described as finished
  • Georgia Southern University is always named in full
  • Demo screens use the fictional Oakhaven name and invented data
